Inquiry Inbox, quotation work, and PO review in one first screen

This internal portal prototype is shaped for the practical export path first. The first screen focuses on the three places where commercial follow-up starts to become execution: new inquiries, quotation handling, and customer PO review.

Inquiry Inbox

New buyer demand that needs categorization and commercial judgment

Use this column to scan the latest inquiries, identify which ones are worth quoting, and decide where category, market, or cooperation mode still need clarification.

Quotation Workspace

Quotations, owner follow-up, and portal handoff readiness

This view keeps quotations visible with customer, version, validity, and owner context, so the team can move faster from inquiry review into PI and customer confirmation.

PO Review Queue

Customer PO files that need business confirmation before execution

This queue is where uploaded PO files are checked against quotation, PI, quantity, packaging, and trade terms before the order is formally handed over to execution.

Workflow Gates

Credit, deposit, manager release, and material planning stay visible as separate queues

These queues separate commercial approval from production release. A PO can be approved for document consistency without automatically pushing the order into production.

Credit Review

Customer risk and payment posture before quotation release

Review customer context, decide payment posture, and keep high-risk customers from moving ahead without finance visibility.

Deposit Confirmation

Deposit proof and payment confirmation before production release

Finance confirms deposit status here and records the commercial proof before the case can move to manager release.

Manager Release

Formal release to production planning after deposit confirmation

This gate keeps document approval separate from the decision to actually release factory planning work.

Inventory & Material

Stock match, missing items, and material preparation list

Production and merchandisers can keep inventory match and purchase-needs preparation visible before execution fully opens.

Cost Review

Manager value review for missing materials and cost impact

Material gaps and related value decisions stay in a dedicated queue before finance sign-off is requested.

Finance Sign-off

Finance sign-off before execution is formally opened

Use this queue to keep PDF sign-off, final review notes, and the formal release into execution under control.

Execution Control

Production, shipment, and balance collection stay connected but separately controlled

After execution opens, the system still keeps production progress, shipment readiness, and BL release under distinct gates instead of merging them into one generic order status.

Production Queue

Orders that are already released and moving on the factory side

Track which cases are in progress, completed, or already ready to hand over into shipping and document preparation.

Shipping & Customs

Booking, trucking, customs, and on-board readiness

Documentation teams can see which orders are ready for booking and which ones are already moving into on-board control.

Balance & BL Release

Final payment follow-up and BL release remain a hard gate

Keep balance collection, BL approval, and release control together so shipping completion does not automatically mean release of documents.
