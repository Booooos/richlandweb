01 · Business Problem

Commercial activity existed, but the operating trail was fragmented

The analysis focused on visibility, ownership, and data integrity—three practical issues that affect follow-up quality in an export sales process.

01

Records lived in separate places

Customer details, quotations, follow-up notes, and order files were handled across messages and documents, without one consistent customer history.

02

Status and ownership were unclear

Teams could not quickly confirm the latest quotation version, next action owner, due date, or whether an order had passed the required approval gate.

03

Exceptions were hard to control

Blank fields, duplicate inquiries, invalid dates, and premature workflow jumps could create rework or move incomplete records downstream.

03 · Workflow

One visible commercial path, with controls at every handoff

The simplified recruiter view below shows the commercial spine. The implementation also preserves detailed internal gates for payment, production release, shipping, and document control.

Stage 01

Inquiry

Capture buyer, market, category, quantity, and cooperation intent.

Stage 02

Customer

Match or create the account and retain contact history.

Stage 03

Follow-Up

Assign an owner, due date, action type, and outcome.

Stage 04

Quotation

Control version, validity, currency, terms, and status.

Stage 05

Order

Confirm PO alignment and enforce approval gates before execution.

Stage 06

Shipment

Track readiness, booking, documents, balance, and release status.

Automation rule exampleIf a follow-up is still open after its due date, mark it overdue and place it in the sales owner's action queue. An order cannot enter production merely because its PO was approved.
04 · Data Model

Relationships make the customer journey traceable

This is the logical CRM model used for analysis and production planning. The current prototype persists operational collections through a Prisma-backed store bridge; normalization is documented as the next database migration.

Quotations1 : many
  • PKquotation_idUUID
  • FKcustomer_idUUID
  • FKinquiry_idUUID

Orders1 : many
  • PKorder_idUUID
  • FKcustomer_idUUID
  • FKquotation_idUUID
  • status / order_valueMIXED
Cardinality: one Customer can have many Follow-Ups, Quotations, and Orders; each Order references the accepted Quotation. Shipment records extend Orders through order_id.
Integrity

Required relationships

Foreign keys prevent quotations or orders from becoming orphaned records and keep the full account history available.

Auditability

Status plus timestamps

Each operational entity includes created and updated timestamps; critical workflow actions create a timeline or audit event.

Validation

Controlled values

Required fields, status sets, valid date ranges, unique business keys, and currency-safe values reduce downstream rework.
